# IT490 Meeting Minutes Template

Use this template for team meetings, milestone planning, troubleshooting sessions, and major project decisions. Meeting minutes should be specific enough that someone can understand what changed, who owns the next work, and where supporting evidence lives.

For short check-ins, prioritize decisions, blockers, action items, and evidence links. The notes do not need to be long; they need to be clear enough that the team can act on them later.
